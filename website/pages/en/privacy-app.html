Privacy policy for the app

This is a translation for convenience. The German Datenschutzerklärung is the legally binding version. This policy applies to the app GhulSuite on Android and Windows. This website has a privacy policy of its own.

In short: GhulSuite sends no data at all to the developer. There is no account, no server, no telemetry, no crash reports in the background, no advertising and no analytics. Your content is encrypted on your device and stored only where you put it yourself: on your device and in the suite file wherever you save it (for example on a USB stick). On its own the app makes only two connections, the update check and, if you switch it on, the leak check. Both are listed in section 4 below and can be switched off.

2. Data on your device

Passwords, 2FA codes, documents, events and settings are kept in encrypted files (Argon2id, XChaCha20-Poly1305; opened only with the master password and a security key or key file). Every document in GhulDocs is encrypted with a key of its own; names and folders are only in the encrypted index. For the optional unlock by fingerprint or app PIN, the app uses the system's protected key store; the app never sees fingerprints.

Scanning documents (Android): the app uses the camera itself, not the system's camera app. Photos and pages stay in memory until the finished PDF is encrypted in GhulDocs; if you leave the scan or the app locks, they are deleted. Only the encrypted PDF is stored.

The suite file (.ghlzip): the device keeps an encrypted working copy of your suite. The app writes it as a file only where you save it, for example on a USB stick or in a folder of your choice. The file is fully encrypted and sealed, the documents in it included; the app only remembers where it is (on Android as a permission for this one file, on Windows as a path). When you open another suite file, the app deletes the device data of the previous state (quick unlock).

Error report: under Options → About → Error report the app shows a report (app and core version, device and system version, the last crash report if any, no vault contents). The app does not send it. You decide whether to copy it, save it or send it to the developer with another app (for example your mail app). If you send it by e-mail, the privacy policy of the website applies (contact by e-mail).

3. What you hand out of the app yourself

The app passes data to other apps or places only when you explicitly do so. What happens there is up to you or the other app:

  • Export (documents, calendar, entries): the file is saved where you choose; for unencrypted exports the app asks first.
  • Open with app (GhulDocs): the chosen app gets a temporary copy of the document, deleted as soon as GhulSuite locks.
  • Share (e.g. an event as .ics, a vault to hand on): through the system's share menu to the app you choose.
  • Where the suite file is saved: if you choose a folder that another program copies to a cloud, that program's terms apply; the file is encrypted and sealed there. The app itself sends it nowhere.

6. Storage period and deletion

Data stays until you delete it. Documents in the GhulDocs trash stay until you empty the trash. Uninstalling the app makes the system remove its data on the device; you delete saved suite files (for example on a USB stick) and exported files yourself.

7. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20), to object (Art. 21) and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Art. 77). As the controller receives no data from the app, most data is with you alone: the app offers export, saving as a suite file and deletion for it.
