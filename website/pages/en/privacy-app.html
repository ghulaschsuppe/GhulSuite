Privacy policy for the app

This is a translation for convenience. The German Datenschutzerklärung is the legally binding version. This policy applies to the app GhulSuite on Android and Windows. This website has a privacy policy of its own.

In short: GhulSuite sends no data at all to the developer. There is no account, no telemetry, no crash reports in the background, no advertising and no analytics. Your content is encrypted on your device and stored only where you set it up: on your own server. The few connections to other services are all listed below.

The controller receives no personal data through the use of the app. They are responsible for the design of the app and the connections it makes on its own (section 4).

2. Data on your device

Passwords, 2FA codes, files, mails, events and settings are kept in encrypted files (Argon2id, XChaCha20-Poly1305; opened only with the master password and a security key or key file). For the optional unlock by fingerprint or app PIN, the app uses the system's protected key store; the app never sees fingerprints. An error report is only stored on the device and only passed on if you copy or save it yourself.

3. Connections you set up yourself

You or the respective provider are responsible for these connections; the app makes them on your behalf:

  • Your server (SFTP, FTPS or the PHP server module) for sync, files, the mail archive and share links. Only encrypted data is stored there. The server's operator sees your IP address and access times.
  • Your mail providers (IMAP and SMTP, encrypted with TLS only). Their privacy policies apply.
  • Share links: whoever opens a link from you fetches it from your server; the key is only in the part of the link that is never sent to the server.

6. Storage period and deletion

Data stays until you delete it. Uninstalling the app makes the system remove its data on the device; you delete files on your own server and in your suite folder yourself.

7. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20), to object (Art. 21) and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Art. 77). As the controller receives no data from the app, most data is with you alone: the app offers export, backup and deletion for it.
