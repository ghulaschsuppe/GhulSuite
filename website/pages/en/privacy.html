2. Visiting the website and server logs

The website is hosted by {{v.hoster_en}}. When you open a page, your browser necessarily sends data that the server stores in log files:

  • IP address of the requesting device
  • date and time of the request
  • page or file requested, amount of data sent and status code
  • the page visited before (referrer), if your browser sends it
  • browser and operating system (user agent)

Purpose: delivering the website, stable and secure operation, detecting and fending off attacks. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR; the legitimate interest is a secure and working website. Storage period: the logs are kept only as long as needed for these purposes and are then deleted or anonymised by the host. They are not combined with other data or analysed for advertising.

The host processes this data on our behalf under a data processing agreement according to Art. 28 GDPR.

3. Encrypted connection

The website is delivered over HTTPS (TLS) only, so data you send to the site cannot be read by third parties.

4. No cookies, local storage in your browser

This website sets no cookies. It keeps at most two entries in your browser's local storage:

EntryContent and purpose
gs-notice"1" once you closed the privacy notice with "Got it", so it does not appear on every visit.
gs-lang"de" or "en": the language chosen. On the first visit of the German start page with a browser not set to German, it is set to "en" and you are taken to the English page once.

These entries never leave your browser, are not sent to the server and contain no identifier. They are strictly necessary for functions you ask for (§ 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG); no consent is needed for them. You can delete them at any time in your browser's settings.

5. Contact by e-mail

If you write an e-mail, your address and the content of your message are processed to answer it. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR where a contract or its preparation is concerned, otherwise Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (answering your request). The message is deleted once it has been dealt with, unless legal retention duties apply.

6. Error reports from the app

Through report.php, this website accepts error reports that someone explicitly sends from the app (report, note, an e-mail address for questions if entered). They are forwarded by e-mail to the controller and not stored on the server. Against mass abuse, the script counts for one day how many reports came from one address; for this the IP address is only kept as a hash (with a random value that changes daily) and deleted after one day at most. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR (consent by sending) and, for the protection against abuse, Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. Details are in the privacy policy of the app.

7. Links to GitHub and downloads

Source code, bug reports and the app's download files are hosted at GitHub (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). This website embeds nothing from GitHub; only when you click a link to GitHub or download a file does your browser connect to GitHub. GitHub then receives your IP address and the usual browser data; the GitHub privacy statement applies. GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, which allows a transfer to the USA (Art. 45 GDPR).

The version details on this site (version number, date, file sizes) are filled in when the site is uploaded. Nothing is fetched from GitHub when you visit the site.

8. No sharing, no automated decisions

Personal data is not sold and not passed on to third parties, except to the host as processor (section 2) or where the law requires it. There is no automated decision-making or profiling.

9. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (Art. 21). An informal e-mail to the address above is enough.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), for example the one where you live or where the controller is based.
